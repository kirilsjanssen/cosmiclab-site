ScrRec

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ScrRec Android application (“ScrRec”, “the app”), developed by CosmicLab Indicators (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the app.

1. What ScrRec does

ScrRec is a screen recording and video editing application. Depending on the features you choose, the app may access screen content, microphone audio, internal device audio available to Android screen capture, the front camera for Facecam, and media files that you select for editing.

2. Screen recordings, audio, camera and media files

Screen recordings, captured audio, Facecam video, imported media and edited projects are processed on your device for the purpose of recording, editing, saving and exporting your content.

ScrRec does not upload your screen recordings, microphone recordings, Facecam video, imported media, or edited project files to our own servers as part of the app’s normal operation.

3. Permissions

ScrRec may request Android permissions or system access for features you choose to use, including:

  • Screen capture / MediaProjection: to record the content displayed on your screen.
  • Microphone: to record microphone audio when you enable microphone recording.
  • Camera: to show and record the optional Facecam overlay.
  • Media / storage access: to let you import, save, edit and export media files where required by Android.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep recording active and provide recording controls while the app is running in the background.

You can deny or revoke optional permissions in Android settings. Some features will not work without the permissions needed for those features.

4. Advertising

The free version of ScrRec may display advertising provided by Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, app interaction data, diagnostics and ad performance information in order to provide, measure, secure and personalize ads where permitted by applicable law and your consent choices.

Where required, ScrRec may show a consent message using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized advertising or related processing takes place.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: Google – How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Data we collect ourselves

ScrRec does not require a ScrRec account and we do not operate a ScrRec cloud account system. We do not intentionally collect your screen recordings, video projects, microphone recordings, Facecam recordings, contacts, messages, passwords or personal documents on our own servers.

6. Local app data

ScrRec may store local settings and project-related information on your device, such as recording preferences, editor state, ad-frequency counters and other app configuration required for normal operation. This information remains on the device unless Android, Google Play services or another service you use handles it independently.

7. Third-party services

ScrRec may use third-party services provided by Google, including:

  • Google Play services
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Google Play distribution and app integrity services

These services may process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Children

ScrRec is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through our own servers. Age-related advertising and consent requirements may be applied according to Google Play, Google Mobile Ads and applicable law.

9. Data security

We design ScrRec so that recording and editing content is handled locally on the device wherever practical. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit our own data collection and do not operate a server that receives users’ recorded screen content as part of normal app functionality.

10. Data retention and deletion

Recorded videos, exported files and project data stored by ScrRec on your device remain under your control. You can delete recordings and projects from the app or your device. Uninstalling ScrRec may remove app-private local data, subject to Android and backup behavior.

Advertising-related data processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google’s own policies and user controls.

11. International processing

Third-party providers such as Google may process information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to their applicable safeguards and privacy policies.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ScrRec features, legal requirements or third-party services change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.
