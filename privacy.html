Privacy Policy – CosmiClab VPN

CosmiClab VPN provides VPN access using WireGuard technology.

We do not collect or store personal user data such as:

  • browsing history
  • traffic content
  • DNS queries

The app processes limited technical data:

  • device identifier (device_id)
  • session status
  • connection timestamps
  • boost usage count

This data is used only to operate the service.

We do not sell or share data with third parties.
