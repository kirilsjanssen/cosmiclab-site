Delete Your Data

Users can request deletion of their data associated with their device identifier. This includes technical and usage-related data. No account is required to use the app.

How to request deletion

You can request data deletion by contacting us:

📩 Email: cosmiclab.support@gmail.com

👉 Telegram: @KirilsSZ

What data will be deleted

  • Device identifier (Device ID)
  • VPN session data
  • Traffic usage statistics

Processing time

Data will be deleted within 24–72 hours after receiving the request.

Data retention

Some minimal technical logs may be temporarily retained for security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance purposes, and are automatically deleted.
